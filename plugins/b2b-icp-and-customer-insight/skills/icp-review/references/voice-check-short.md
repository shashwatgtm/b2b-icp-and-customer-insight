# Short voice check (carried inside this skill so it stands alone)

Run this on any sentence the skill writes for a reader: a finding, a hypothesis, a summary. It does not apply to quotes copied from interviews, notes or pages, which stay exactly as given, and it does not apply to the user's own wording unless the user asked for a rewrite.

## Write
- Plain spoken English at the reading level of the named reader. Indian English and regional spelling are fine; keep the user's choice.
- Prose in sentences of normal length. Tables for data and comparisons.
- Specific over general: the user's figure, the segment, the role, the date.
- Calm and grounded; written from inside the problem, not above it.

## Never write
- Long or short dashes (and spaced hyphens used as dashes). Hyphens inside words are fine.
- Exclamation marks. Emoji.
- Staccato fragments of one to three words ("No guesswork. Just data.").
- The contrast pattern "It is not X. It is Y." and neat closing aphorisms.
- Hype or fear: "massive", "explosive", catastrophe openers.
- Coined labels the reader would not use.
- These words and phrases: game-changer, leverage, dive in, delve, tapestry, elevate, unlock, seamless, genuinely, honestly, straightforward, revolutionize, supercharge, harness, empower, navigate (for abstract things), in today's world, ever-evolving, next-level, it's important to note, testament to, move the needle, here's the thing, groundbreaking, transformative, comprehensive, robust, holistic, synergy, paradigm shift.

## Check
Dashes and these words are checked by reading the draft once more before it goes out. Where a script in this skill prints text, its own output is already plain.
