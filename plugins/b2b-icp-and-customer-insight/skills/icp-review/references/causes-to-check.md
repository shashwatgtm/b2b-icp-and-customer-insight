# Causes to check when a metric is behind target

These are checks, not findings. Pick the ones the user's own notes point to and say which note points there. When no note points to a cause, tie each check to a split the user's own data allows (by segment, by size band, by buyer role, by source, inside against outside the ICP, wins against losses) and name the split in the user's words; a check no split can test goes last, with the field to add. Never present a cause as the answer without the user's evidence. No statistic or benchmark is given here.

## Average contract value below target
Checks: selling to smaller companies than the ICP names; buyers without budget for the full scope; the deal led by a user rather than the budget owner; discounting to close; packaging that caps the deal (no tier for larger buyers).
Actions to consider: tighten the size part of the ICP; bring the budget owner in earlier; review discount approvals; test a tier for larger buyers.
Services variant (ITeS and other services): small scopes or statements of work, proposals priced against other vendors, engagements started as pilots that never grew. Network equipment variant (telecom): trials that stay at lab scale, single site orders.

## Sales cycle longer than target
Checks: unclear value for the budget owner; more reviewers than expected (security, procurement, legal); no champion inside; competing priorities; long pilots without exit criteria.
Actions to consider: name the champion and signer early (see the buying role skill if installed); agree pilot exit criteria; start security and procurement reviews in parallel.

## Win rate below target
Checks: weak qualification (deals outside the ICP entering the pipeline); losing to doing nothing; unclear difference from alternatives; price against the value the buyer sees.
Actions to consider: add disqualifiers from the loss reasons; quantify the cost of doing nothing with the buyer's own figures; hand competitor work to competitive-intelligence in b2b-sales-enablement.

## Churn above target
Checks: customers outside the ICP; slow onboarding or time to value; the champion left; the use case was secondary or experimental; a better fit alternative.
Actions to consider: compare churn of customers inside and outside the ICP in the user's own data; tighten the use case part of the ICP; hand account saves to b2b-customer-success.
Services variant: engagements not renewed, team changes on the delivery side, scope moved to another vendor or in house. Network variant: sites not expanded after the first rollout.

## NPS below target
Checks: which segment gives the low scores; whether detractors sit outside the ICP; support or onboarding issues; promises in the sale the product does not keep.
Actions to consider: split NPS by ICP fit in the user's data; interview detractors (customer-interviews skill).

## Sector words
Use the measure names of the user's vertical (references/sector-notes.md): contract value for services, cost per shipment for logistics, uptime for telecom. Do not call an engagement a subscription, and do not use product words (tier, onboarding, time to value, trial) for a services firm; use scope, transition, delivery and proposal instead.
