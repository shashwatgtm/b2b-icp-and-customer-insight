# PMF check method

## Against your own targets, never a published range
Product market fit is judged here only against the targets the team sets for itself (from its plan, its board or its own history). No published range, benchmark or "good churn for SaaS" figure is used, because such ranges differ by source, segment and year and are rarely sourced. If the user asks for one, the skill asks for their target or for a source they trust, and uses that source only with its link and date.

## The dimensions
| Dimension | What it shows | Better | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Churn (%) | customers or revenue lost in the period | lower | say whether it is logo or revenue churn, monthly or annual, and keep the same basis for value and target |
| NPS | how many customers would recommend | higher | points from -100 to 100; never compared with a percentage |
| LTV to CAC | value of a customer against the cost to win one | higher | derived from LTV and CAC when both are given and CAC is above 0 |
| Retention (%) | customers or revenue kept | higher | net revenue retention can exceed 100%; say which kind |
| Activation (%) | new customers reaching first value | higher | not applicable for services, connectivity and investment businesses: use the adoption measure buyers use (for example sites live, engagements renewed) |
| Your own | any measure the team tracks (revenue growth, pilots converted, usage) | the user says | a negative value keeps its minus sign |

## Reading the result
- "k of n scored dimensions meet your own targets" is the result. There is no stage label (no "strong" or "emerging" fit), because a label would need a range the skill does not have.
- The dimension furthest behind (largest percentage gap) is where to look first; NPS is reported in points beside it.
- The base of a gap is the size of the current value, sign ignored. A gap from a negative or near zero value is large; also state it in points or units (for example "32 points of growth to recover").
- Met or behind is decided on the raw values: a shortfall of under half a percent is "less than 1%" behind, never met.
- A dimension with no target is not scored. Ask the user for a target once; never fill one in.

## What to check when a dimension is behind (checks, not findings)
- Churn behind: churn by segment and by fit with the ICP; time to first value; the champion leaving. Saving accounts is b2b-customer-success.
- NPS behind: which segment gives low scores; detractor interviews (customer-interviews in this plugin).
- LTV to CAC behind: which channel's CAC is highest; contract value by segment (icp-review in this plugin).
- Retention behind: expansion against contraction; which segment shrinks.
- Activation behind: the step where new customers stall; whether the activation event is the right one.

## Customer feedback the user pastes
Quotes stay word for word. Each is sorted by reading the whole line (never by a single word inside it): a story (what happened), a result the customer states (with their figure), a claim (the team's or a page's words), or friction (a problem). A friction line points to a check, never to a verdict on fit.
